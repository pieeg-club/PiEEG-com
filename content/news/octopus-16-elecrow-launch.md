Today we're excited to announce that **Octopus 16 is officially available on Elecrow.** High-density, wireless biosignal acquisition just got a whole lot more portable.

## 16 Channels, No Wires, No Lab

Octopus 16 brings 16-channel biosignal acquisition to the palm of your hand. Built on the **Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3**, it streams EEG, EMG, and ECG data wirelessly over Bluetooth—no single-board computer or bulky wired setup required.

At its core are Texas Instruments **ADS131M08** analog front-ends, delivering high-quality **24-bit** biosignal data across all 16 channels.

### Why Octopus 16

- **16 full channels** for rich spatial coverage in a compact, wearable form factor
- **Wireless Bluetooth streaming** — pairs like any Bluetooth device
- **24-bit resolution** per channel for research-grade signal capture
- **Battery-powered** operation isolates the device from mains noise
- **PiEEG-Server integrated** — visualize your data directly in the browser
- **PiEEG Cloud compatible** — connect right from your browser, zero install

**No gel required. No lab required. Just neural data, wirelessly.**

## Built for Builders

Octopus 16 is ideal for researchers, students, and developers building advanced BCI prototypes, wireless neural-interface applications, and multi-channel embedded setups. Firmware for the XIAO ESP32-S3 flashes in seconds, and the open-source Python SDK includes tools for FFT analysis and real-time signal visualization.

And because Octopus 16 is fully compatible with **[PiEEG Cloud](https://cloud.pieeg.com/lobby)**, you can connect and start streaming straight from your browser over Web Bluetooth—no installation required.
