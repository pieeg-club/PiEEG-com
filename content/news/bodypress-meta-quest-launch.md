We're excited to announce that **BodyPress** is now available on the **Meta Quest** platform!

Experience your neural data in an entirely new dimension. BodyPress for Meta Quest brings the same AI-powered wellness companion you love from mobile, now optimized for virtual reality.

## Download Now

**[Get BodyPress on Meta Quest](https://www.meta.com/experiences/bodypress/1271355469389420)**

## What Makes This Special

### Immersive Neural Visualization
Step into your brain's activity like never before. BodyPress in VR transforms raw EEG signals into spatial, 3D representations that surround you.

### Seamless PiEEG XR Integration
Works perfectly with [PiEEG XR](/xr), our neural face interface for Meta Quest. Combine facial EMG control with real-time brain monitoring for the complete picture of your mental and physical state.

### Privacy-First, Offline-Capable
All neural data processing happens locally on your headset. No cloud uploads, no external servers—just you and your data.

### AI-Powered Insights in VR
The same warm, first-person narratives from mobile BodyPress, now rendered in an immersive VR environment. Your body still writes the journal—now you can read it in virtual space.

## Key Features

**Real-Time EEG Monitoring:**
- Live signal quality indicators
- Per-channel electrode health
- Mental state visualization in 3D

**VR-Native Interface:**
- Spatial audio cues for signal quality
- Hand tracking support
- Comfort-first UI placement

**Cross-Platform Compatibility:**
- Works with all PiEEG hardware (8-32 channels)
- PiEEG XR facial interface integration
- Meta Quest 2, Quest 3, Quest Pro support

## How It Works

1. **Put on your Meta Quest** and launch BodyPress
2. **Connect your PiEEG device** via Bluetooth
3. **Start monitoring** your neural data in immersive VR

For PiEEG XR users, the facial EMG interface works automatically—just wear it and go.
