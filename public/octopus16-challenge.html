    The hardware

    What they're
    building with

    Octopus 16 is a wireless 16-channel biosignal board on the Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3, with dual 24-bit ADS131M08 front-ends and dry pogo-pin electrodes.

    Channels
    16
    Resolution
    24-bit · ADS131M08
    Platform
    XIAO ESP32-S3
    Signals
    EEG · EMG · ECG
