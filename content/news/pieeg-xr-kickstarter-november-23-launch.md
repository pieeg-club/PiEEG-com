The countdown is set: **[PiEEG XR](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/42415505/pieeg-xr-neural-face-interface-for-spatial-computing)** officially launches on **Kickstarter on November 23, 2026**.

Over the past several months, user questions and developer requests pushed us to put PiEEG XR through demanding real-world trials outside the lab—refining mechanical fit, signal stability during movement, enclosure manufacturing, and companion software before opening orders.

## Field-Tested Across Continents

- **Tokyo, Japan — Independent Media Evaluation:** Japan's leading VR publication, Mogura VR (MoguLive), put PiEEG XR through intensive multi-week testing across spatial computing scenarios and delivered candid ergonomic and workflow feedback.
- **Madrid, Spain — Live VR Goalkeeper Training:** Partnering with **BeFootball**, we recorded live neural and physiological signals during active VR goalkeeper training sessions to evaluate signal integrity under rapid head and body movement.
- **New Companion Software — BodyMove:** Pair a standard camera with your headset stream to capture full-body movement kinematics alongside live EEG and facial sEMG on a single synchronized timeline.

## From 3D-Printed Prototypes to Factory Enclosures

Early testers will recognize the grey 3D-printed housing clipped to our development headsets—iterated layer by layer in-house as we refined strap geometry and cable routing. Ahead of launch, our **production-grade factory enclosures** have arrived.

![PiEEG XR Factory Production Enclosure](/news-images/pieeg-xr-factory-enclosure.jpg)

Finished in clean matte white to match standalone VR headsets, the injection-ready production shell is **30% smaller**, structurally stiffer, and clips directly onto the headset strap for plug-and-stream operation. Available in **Standard (8-channel)** and **Academic (16-channel, powered by IronBCI-16)** configurations, its soft silicone sensor mask brings live brain and facial muscle activity into VR without face-tracking cameras.
