**Aura** is a wristband for XR. Four EMG sensors, an accelerometer, and a gyroscope. Capture gestures and movement, then send them into VR as they happen.

No cameras. No controllers to hold. Muscle activity and kinematics, from the wrist.

**[Get early access →](https://aura.pieeg.com/#signup)**

## Muscle and motion, in one band

EMG reads intent at the forearm. The IMU tracks how the wrist actually moves. Together they give XR a body, not just a pointer.

### Four dry EMG contacts

Dry contacts sit around the wrist and pick up forearm activity: grip, pinch, flick, rest. Muscle activity, not cameras. No occlusion.

### Accelerometer and gyro, fused

The puck holds a 6-axis IMU. Orientation, flicks, and continuous movement, timestamped with the EMG stream.

### Live to XR

Gestures and kinematics leave the wrist as a live stream. The headset gets muscle and motion, together.

### Wristband form

Textile strap, four contacts, one puck. Wear it and move.
