Super happy to say that finally our app, **BodyPress**, with full support for PiEEG devices, is now live on **Google Play**! 

This one wasn't easy, but we did it. 💪

You can now read data from all biodata from PiEEG devices, as well as **PiEEG XR** (EEG in VR), directly on your mobile phone. Simple, comfortable, and easy.

## What's in This Release

### New Home Screen
A categorized hub to quickly reach every feature—no more hunting through menus to access your neural data streams.

### Companion Mode
An ambient view of your mental state in real time. Think of it as a calm, persistent window into your brain's activity while you go about your day.

### Electrode Health Indicators
Clearer signal-quality feedback so you know instantly when electrode contact needs adjustment. No more guessing why your signal looks noisy.

### PiEEG Server Screen
Built-in dashboard access for advanced users running local PiEEG Server instances. Full control without leaving the mobile app.

### Improved Bluetooth Scanning
More reliable device connections with enhanced BLE discovery. Connects faster and maintains stable sessions across all PiEEG hardware—from 8-channel prototypes to 32-channel professional systems.

## Why This Matters

**Your body writes a journal every day, and now, wherever you are, you can read it.**

BodyPress isn't just a data viewer—it's an AI-powered wellness companion that transforms raw sensor readings into first-person narratives. Instead of staring at disconnected metrics, you read a warm, insightful story written as if your body is speaking to you.

### What BodyPress Collects

The app synthesizes multiple data streams into one cohesive picture:

**Brain Activity** (via PiEEG devices):
- Live EEG signals from all channels
- Signal quality per electrode
- Mental state indicators

**Health Metrics** (via Health Connect):
- Steps, distance, calories
- Heart rate, HRV (RMSSD, SDNN)
- Sleep stages and duration
- Workout sessions

**Environmental Context**:
- Temperature, air quality (AQI)
- UV index, humidity, weather
- Location (read once for weather, then discarded)

**Bluetooth Heart Rate**:
- Real-time waveform from Polar, Wahoo, Garmin straps
- BPM and RR intervals

### How It Works

1. **COLLECT** — Data from sensors, Health Connect, Bluetooth, and environmental APIs
2. **SYNTHESIZE** — AI finds patterns and correlations across all signals  
3. **NARRATE** — A first-person journal entry written from your body's perspective
4. **DISCOVER** — Insights emerge over time: poor sleep before elevated heart rate, cleaner-air days with better focus

## Privacy & Security

- GPS read once per capture for weather, then immediately discarded (no location history stored)
- Health data is read-only — BodyPress never writes to Health Connect
- Encrypted connections, no tracking, no data selling
- Open-source codebase on GitHub
