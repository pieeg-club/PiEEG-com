**PiEEG XR** has been featured in a comprehensive independent review by **MoguLive (Mogura VR)**, one of Japan's foremost virtual reality and spatial computing publications.

Rather than a brief show-floor demo, the MoguLive editorial team spent **two months testing PiEEG XR** in real-world conditions across multiple VR applications before publishing their findings. The article is completely independent—neither sponsored nor commissioned as paid media.

## Real-World Feedback Ahead of Launch

Extended external testing is one of the best ways to stress-test wearable neurotechnology outside the lab. Over the two-month evaluation, MoguLive examined how a non-invasive biosignal interface fits onto a standalone VR headset, how EEG and facial muscle activity behave during active head movement, and what developers and VR enthusiasts can realistically build with live physiological streams.

Their detailed observations—alongside our recent sports-training sessions in Europe—directly informed hardware fit refinements, enclosure ergonomics, and setup workflow improvements leading into the upcoming PiEEG XR release.
